Two services

Two services, side by side, because they answer different questions.

Some patients are referred for a comprehensive perioperative consultation before surgery — often without us being the anaesthetist on the day. Others meet us as their anaesthetist for a scheduled procedure. Both sit under one practice, one philosophy.

Service 01 By referral · Consultation

Perioperative Consultation

Delivered by a specialist anaesthetist with a special interest in perioperative care.

A structured, unhurried assessment for patients facing complex or higher-risk surgery. We map the whole journey — medical, functional, personal — and coordinate the team needed to arrive at surgery in the best possible shape.

What the consultation covers

  • Detailed risk stratification & shared decision-making
  • Medication review and optimisation
  • Cardiac, respiratory & functional reserve assessment
  • Prehabilitation planning
  • Anaesthetic strategy recommendation for the treating team

Coordinated with

  • Referring surgeons
  • Physicians & cardiologists
  • Geriatricians
  • Physiotherapists
  • Dietitians & allied health

Please note: In this pathway we are not usually the anaesthetist on the day of surgery. Our role is the consult, the plan, and the coordination — your surgical and anaesthetic team on the day is briefed with a clear written recommendation.

Service 02 Booked lists · Anaesthesia

Specialist Anaesthesia

For a defined group of surgeons and procedures, we provide the anaesthetic care directly — from pre-op review, through theatre, to recovery. Modern techniques, chosen for the patient in front of us.

What this involves

  • Pre-operative assessment & consent
  • General, regional and sedation techniques
  • Opioid-sparing recovery where clinically appropriate
  • Post-op review and pain management

Typical case mix

  • General surgery
  • Orthopaedic surgery
  • Endoscopy & day procedures
  • Selected higher-risk patients

About

The specialist behind the practice.

A/Prof Joel Symons MBBS, MMed, GChPOM, FANZCA

Specialist Anaesthetist · Associate Professor, University of Melbourne

Perioperative Partners is led by Associate Professor Joel Symons — a Melbourne specialist anaesthetist recognised locally and internationally for his contributions to perioperative medicine education.

Joel is a Staff Specialist Anaesthetist at The Alfred and Associate Professor at the University of Melbourne. His practice sits within specialist anaesthesia, with a particular focus on the care of complex and higher-risk surgical patients.

He is a Board Member and Chair of the Education and Assessment Committee for the ANZCA Chapter of Perioperative Medicine and a member of the ANZCA Perioperative Medicine Special Interest Group Executive.

Joel co-developed the Monash Short Course and Master of Perioperative Medicine programmes, co-edited Perioperative Medicine for the Junior Clinician (Wiley), and is Editor of the Perioperative Medicine Journal (Springer Nature). His clinical and research interests span perioperative risk assessment, patient blood management, and the education of the next generation of perioperative clinicians.

Pathways

How each service unfolds.

Perioperative consultation

  1. Step 1

    Referral received

    From the referring surgeon, with the proposed procedure and relevant history.

  2. Step 2

    Comprehensive consult

    Risk, function, medications, patient priorities. Investigations arranged if needed.

  3. Step 3

    Onward referrals to physicians, geriatricians, physio, dietitians as appropriate.

  4. Step 4

    Written plan issued

    Clear recommendations to the treating surgical and anaesthetic team ahead of the day.

  1. Step 1

    List confirmed

    Case scheduled with the referring surgeon and the treating hospital.

  2. Step 2

    Pre-op review

    A focused review of the patient's health and anaesthetic considerations before theatre.

  3. Step 3

    Anaesthesia delivered

    Safe, evidence-based anaesthetic tailored to the patient and the procedure.

  4. Step 4

    Recovery & handover

    Post-op review, pain management, and clear handover to ward and surgical team.

The network

Not a solo practice. A small hub connected to the right people.

The perioperative consultation exists because complex surgery is rarely a single-specialty problem. A patient with unstable angina needs a cardiologist. A frail older patient needs a geriatrician. A deconditioned patient needs a physio. A patient losing weight needs a dietitian.

Our role is to see the whole picture at one long, careful appointment — and then to pull in the colleagues who make the difference before theatre. Where we don't administer the anaesthetic ourselves, we hand over a clear, actionable plan that the treating anaesthetist can rely on.

The extended team

The colleagues we bring in.

Complex perioperative care is a team sport. Depending on the patient, a shared plan may draw on any of the following — arranged and coordinated as part of the consultation.

  • 01

    General & perioperative physicians

    Multi-system optimisation — diabetes, renal impairment, complex medication regimens, anticoagulation, and unresolved medical questions before theatre.

For referrers

Referral in one page.

Who to refer

Adult patients with complex comorbidities, older patients considering major surgery, patients where the decision to proceed is itself uncertain, and any patient whose surgical team would value a shared perioperative plan.

How to refer

Consultations are by invited referrer only. Established surgical partners have been provided with a private referral link and instructions directly.

What you receive

A structured consultation letter with risk assessment, optimisation plan, anaesthetic strategy recommendation, and onward referrals arranged — usually within one working day of the consultation.
