Privacy

How we look after your information.

Perioperative Partners handles sensitive personal and health information. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, how we store it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Who this applies to

This statement covers everyone whose information passes through Perioperative Partners:

  • Patients booked for a perioperative consultation or anaesthesia
  • Referring surgeons and their practice staff
  • Anyone using this website or our clinician portal at portal.perioperativepartners.com.au

We are a Melbourne-based medical practice and we handle personal and health information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

From patients

  • Your name, date of birth, contact email and phone number
  • Details of the surgery or procedure you are booked for
  • Your medical history, current medications, allergies, and answers to the questionnaires we send you
  • Any documents you upload (test results, imaging reports, previous consultation letters)
  • Appointment details (when you were seen, cancellations, reschedules)

From surgeons

  • Your name, practice name, contact email and phone number
  • Referral letters you send and any documents attached to them
  • A record of which patients you have referred and when

From website visitors

The public marketing website (perioperativepartners.com.au) uses no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers. If you send us a referral email, we receive whatever information you choose to include.

Why we collect it

We only use your information for reasons directly connected to your care:

  • To review your history before your consultation and give you a safe anaesthetic plan
  • To confirm and remind you about appointments
  • To write to your surgeon, GP, and other treating clinicians about your plan of care
  • To meet our legal obligations for medical record-keeping (typically at least 7 years for adults, longer for minors)

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for marketing, and we do not share it with insurers, employers, or third parties beyond the clinicians involved in your care — except where the law requires it (for example, a court order or mandatory reporting).

How we keep it secure

In transit

All connections to our website and portal are encrypted with modern TLS (HTTPS). Referral emails sent to referrals@perioperativepartners.com.au travel over standard email infrastructure and are protected in transit where the sending server supports it. If you need higher assurance than standard email provides, please use the portal or an encrypted transfer service you already trust.

At rest

Inside the portal, patient records and questionnaire responses are stored in an encrypted database. Every document uploaded to the portal — referral letters, imaging reports, consent forms — is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to disk. Nobody can read those files without the decryption key, which only the portal software holds.

Where it lives

The portal is currently hosted on managed cloud infrastructure in a United States region. Data at rest is encrypted as described above; connections are always encrypted end-to-end. We are actively working on moving the portal to Australian-based hosting; when that migration is complete this page will be updated.

Access controls

Different people see different things:

  • Patients log in with a private link we email you, plus your date of birth. You can only see your own information.
  • Surgeons log in with a username and password. You can only see the referrals you have submitted.
  • Clinic staff (A/Prof Symons and directly supervised administrative staff) can see everything in the portal — this is necessary to run the clinic.

Backups are made regularly, encrypted, and retained only as long as needed for disaster recovery.

Who we share information with

We share information only with people directly involved in your care:

  • Your referring surgeon and their rooms
  • Your GP, where relevant
  • Other treating specialists you have consented to us contacting (for example, a cardiologist)
  • The hospital or day-procedure centre where your surgery is booked

We do not send your information overseas for processing (beyond the incidental fact that the hosting infrastructure is currently in the United States, as noted above). We do not disclose information for research, teaching, or quality-assurance purposes without your specific consent, except where de-identification makes that reasonable and lawful.

How long we keep it

Australian medical record-keeping laws require us to retain clinical records for at least 7 years after your last consultation for adults, and until the patient's 25th birthday for minors. In some cases we may keep records longer where legal, insurance, or clinical governance reasons apply.

Portal accounts that are inactive for extended periods may have their login credentials disabled, but the underlying clinical record is retained for the periods above.

Your rights

Under Australian privacy law you can:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • Ask us to explain how we have used your information
  • Withdraw your consent for us to contact particular clinicians on your behalf
  • Make a complaint if you think we have mishandled your information

To do any of these things, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Cookies and tracking

The public marketing site sets no cookies and uses no tracking. The portal uses a single browser session token to keep you logged in for the length of your session; this token is deleted when you log out or close the browser and is never shared with any other party.

Changes to this statement

If we make a material change to how we handle your information — for example, moving the portal to a new host or adding a new integration — we will update this page and note it at the top. The "Last updated" date always reflects the most recent revision.

Getting in touch

Privacy questions or requests

Email: hello@perioperativepartners.com.au

Please write "Privacy" in the subject line so we can route it quickly.

If you have an urgent clinical question — for example, you cannot access the portal before your appointment — email the same address and we will respond as quickly as we can during business hours.
