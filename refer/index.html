Referral

Sending a patient for perioperative consultation.

This page is shared only with established surgical partners. It sets out how to send a patient for perioperative consultation, what to include, and what you'll receive back.

How to send a referral

Please send referrals by secure email to the address below. A structured referral letter on your practice letterhead is preferred.

Referral email

referrals@perioperativepartners.com.au

Compose referral email →

What to include

  • Proposed procedure and provisional surgical date
  • Comorbidities and functional status
  • Current medications, including anticoagulants and antiplatelet agents
  • Recent investigations (ECG, echocardiogram, bloods, imaging)
  • Any prior anaesthetic issues or family history of concern
  • Patient contact details for appointment scheduling

Urgent reviews are accommodated where clinically necessary — please indicate urgency in the subject line.

What you'll receive

A structured consultation letter, usually within one working day of the appointment, covering:

  • Individualised risk assessment
  • Optimisation plan and pre-operative recommendations
  • Anaesthetic strategy recommendation for the treating anaesthetist
  • Onward referrals to physicians, geriatricians, physiotherapists, or dietitians as required — arranged on your patient's behalf

Direct contact

For clinical queries or to discuss a patient before referring, please contact the rooms directly. Contact details have been provided to your practice separately.
